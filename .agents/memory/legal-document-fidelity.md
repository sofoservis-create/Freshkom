---
name: Legal document fidelity
description: How to handle user-supplied Freshkom legal documents and their language versions
---

Treat supplied legal documents as source material to reproduce, not copy to rewrite. Preserve their full wording, effective dates, appendices, language-version notices and any passages that remain untranslated. Keep downloadable originals byte-identical to the uploads.

**Why:** The legal documents are supplied by the business, and changing a phrase, translating mixed-language passages or removing an appendix could change their meaning. Readability improvements must not silently become legal amendments.

**How to apply:** Improve semantic structure, line breaks, navigation and mobile layout around the supplied text. Ask for an updated authoritative document before changing substantive wording or translation. Verify that all source paragraphs appear in the rendered pages and that downloads match the originals.
