---
name: Cookie consent scope
description: Business intent and safeguards for cookie preferences before Google/Meta tools are deployed.
---

The user wants a Slovak/Hungarian cookie banner now and intends to add Google and Meta tracking later. This request authorizes preparing preference controls, not installing or activating those tracking services.

Today's preferences must not be treated as blanket consent for future undisclosed vendors or purposes. Newly added tools and materially changed purposes require updated information and a fresh choice before optional tracking starts.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the tracking tools are not present yet. A banner must describe the current situation truthfully rather than claim active tracking or obtain unspecified future permission.

**How to apply:** Keep cookie preferences separate from contact-form privacy acknowledgement and email/SMS marketing permission. Do not add tags or identifiers just to make the banner appear functional. Preserve the user-supplied legal documents and their original downloads; obtain approval for substantive amendments when tools are actually connected.

Keep the initial banner brief: a short sentence, privacy-policy link, and the three choices. Place short category/status explanations in settings rather than discussing hypothetical future tools in the initial prompt.

**Why:** The user found the longer explanation about inactive tools and future integrations unnecessarily confusing.

**How to apply:** Reduce explanatory copy without hiding information needed for genuine future consent or changing the default-denied behavior.
